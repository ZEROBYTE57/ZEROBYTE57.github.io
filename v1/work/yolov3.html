The problem

The goal of this Intel Unnati industrial training project was an object detector for Indian road scenes, built by implementing YOLOv3 (Darknet-53) in PyTorch rather than calling a ready-made detector.

What I built

  • Implemented YOLOv3 in PyTorch, including the Darknet config parser, the pre-trained weight loader and the detection layers.
  • Wrote the post-processing pipeline: bounding-box decoding, IoU, confidence thresholding and non-maximum suppression.
  • Ran inference on 416×416 Indian Driving Dataset images and timed it against an OpenCV DNN pipeline.
