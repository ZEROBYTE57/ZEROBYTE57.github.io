01 — About

Broken runs forge better models.

I am completing an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (AI and ML) at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham and will graduate in December 2026. My dissertation builds a benchmark to test whether explanations from graph neural networks point to real disease signals or to scanner artefacts.

I enjoy problems that need both first-principles thinking and careful engineering. I break a vague question into testable pieces, build the simplest thing that could work, then try hard to prove it wrong. I read papers to borrow ideas, write clean and reproducible code, and treat evaluation and failure analysis as part of the work, not an afterthought.

M.Tech Dissertation · 2026 In progress ↗

Connectome Explanation Benchmark (CXB)

A benchmark that plants a known connectivity effect into real ABIDE I fMRI connectomes (848 subjects, 19 hospitals), so model explanations can be scored against an exact ground truth.

  • Showed that a train-only scanner shortcut raises the scanner share of the top explanation edges by up to 0.43, while test accuracy falls by only 0.03 to 0.07.
  • Proposed counterfactual site training (CF-Site): +0.15 AUPRC in disease recovery over plain training, matching the best ComBat variant without harmonizing new-site data.
  • PyTorch Geometric
  • Captum
  • nilearn
  • scikit-learn
Kaggle · 2026 In progress ↗

LLM Response Preference Prediction

Predicting which of two LLM answers a human preferred (A, B or tie) on 57K Chatbot Arena battles across 64 LLMs, evaluated on log loss.

  • Analyzed per-model win, loss and tie rates to understand how preferences vary across models.
  • Found and fixed a pipeline bug that broke Kaggle scoring, then shipped a validated baseline.
  • Now adding TF-IDF text features to move beyond model-identity signals.

M.Tech Thesis Completed ↗

SMOTE-Enhanced Multi-View Deep CNN for Mammogram Classification

A 2.24M-parameter multi-view (CC and MLO) CNN for benign and malignant classification on CBIS-DDSM (1,566 patients), with stratified patient-level 70/15/15 splits. Read the thesis.

  • Engineered a SMOTE-based view-completion method that synthesizes missing views by donor-based convex interpolation, growing complete four-view cases from 105 to 1,223.
  • Ran a three-tier experiment (baseline, view completion, hybrid class balancing). The final tier reached 84.02% accuracy, 92.86% recall, 85.25% F1 and 93.24% AUC.
  • Built the full pipeline: CLAHE enhancement, class-imbalance handling, BCE-with-logits training, learning-rate scheduling and early stopping.
  • PyTorch
  • CLAHE
  • SMOTE
  • scikit-learn
Intel Unnati Industrial Training · 2023 Completed ↗

Road Object Detection using YOLOv3

A YOLOv3 (Darknet-53) object detector implemented in PyTorch for Indian road scenes.

  • Implemented YOLOv3 in PyTorch, including the config parser, pre-trained weight loader and detection layers.
  • Wrote the post-processing pipeline: bounding box decoding, IoU, confidence thresholding and non-maximum suppression.
  • Ran inference on 416×416 Indian Driving Dataset images and timed it against an OpenCV DNN pipeline.
